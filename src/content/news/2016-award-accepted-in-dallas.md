Tim Ridge and Jerry Bishop accepted D.R.A.W.'s 2016 Organization of the Year award for the Top End Club on Saturday, at the D.R.A.W. charity auction at the Texas Motorplex in Dallas.

The club started giving to D.R.A.W. only this spring, in memory of Mark Lyle and Ronnie Davis. Two nights at zMAX have raised $2,885 so far. Thank you to Team Skillman for hosting Tim and Jerry, and to everyone who has given.
