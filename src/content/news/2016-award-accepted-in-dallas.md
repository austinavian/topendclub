Tim Ridge and Jerry Bishop accepted D.R.A.W.'s [2016 Organization of the Year](/news/2016-draw-organization-of-the-year/) award for the Top End Club on Saturday, at the D.R.A.W. charity auction at the Texas Motorplex in Dallas. They made the 16-hour drive from North Carolina for it.

## Sending them off

> In just a couple more hours my brothers Tim Ridge and Jerry Bishop will head out on a 16-hour drive bound for the NHRA AAA Texas Fall Nationals. They will be representing the TEC at the D.R.A.W. charity auction and accepting the award for 2016 Organization of the Year. Still blows my mind that we are being recognized for just being us.
>
> Be safe, my brothers, and thanks to Melissa and Allan Lindsey and everyone at Team Skillman for taking care of my boys down there.

<p class="post-source">Rodney Smith, in the club’s Facebook group</p>

The club started giving to D.R.A.W. only this spring, in memory of Mark Lyle and Ronnie Davis. Two nights at zMAX have raised $2,885 so far. Thank you to Team Skillman for hosting Tim and Jerry, and to everyone who has given.
