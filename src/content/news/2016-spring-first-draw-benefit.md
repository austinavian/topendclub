After four years of cooking for whoever came by, the Top End Club tried something new this spring. The club named Saturday night the Mark Lyle and Ronnie Davis Memorial BBQ, put out a donation box, and promised every dollar to D.R.A.W., the Drag Racing Association of Women, in honor of two friends lost this year.

## The numbers

- **Raised on the night** $1,460
- **Given to D.R.A.W. so far** $1,460

## How it started

The idea came together a few weeks before the race, when the club decided to turn its Saturday night party into a memorial BBQ. [Read the announcement](/news/2016-mark-lyle-memorial-bbq/).
