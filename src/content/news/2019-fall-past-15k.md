Live music joined dinner for the fall cookout, and the crowd answered Rodney's challenge from the spring. Saturday night raised $2,560 for D.R.A.W., a new club record, and the all-time total passed $15,000.

## The numbers

- **Raised on the night** $2,560
- **Given to D.R.A.W. so far** $15,142

## From Rodney

> A big thank you to everyone who came out and supported Saturday night. You gave and broke a record with $2,560.00 collected for D.R.A.W. Thanks for your help, and hope you had a great time. We try hard to create a great place to come hang out for a while and give back to our favorite charity. Thanks again to everyone.
>
> BTW, WE BROKE THE $15K MARK. Total to date for D.R.A.W.: $15,142.00!
