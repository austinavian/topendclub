The fall event will be private again, like spring, for sponsors and invited teams and guests. While planning it, the club worried that new COVID-19 restrictions could arrive at any time and leave food, supplies and sponsor money wasted, so it chose a smaller event. The goal stays big: break $20,000 given to D.R.A.W.

## From Rodney

> As we started to plan for this event, we pondered the growing concerns of the COVID situation and were afraid there could be restrictions put in place at any point. We didn't want to plan a full event and be left with food and supplies that couldn't be used, and waste sponsor money that we have been blessed and entrusted with. Hopefully the '22 season will allow us to be back to normal, where we can have the band back and party down.
>
> We really hope everyone helps us break the $20k donation to D.R.A.W. goal. Please be generous as you have been in the past. It's for a great cause, and 100% of this money is put to use for injured drag racers.

<p class="post-source">Rodney Smith, in the club’s Facebook group</p>

The night raised $2,363 and took the club to $20,005. [Read about fall 2021](/news/2021-fall-past-20k/).
