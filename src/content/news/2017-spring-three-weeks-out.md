The spring Mark Lyle and Ronnie Davis Memorial BBQ is three weeks away. The Top End Club will be set up from Thursday morning to Sunday evening, and Saturday night's dinner is free for everyone, with donations going to D.R.A.W.

## From Rodney

> Yes friends, the time is drawing near. Just 3 weeks till we host our Top End Club Mark Lyle and Ronnie Davis Memorial Spring BBQ. Looking forward to seeing everyone again and having another great weekend. Remember the TEC is not just Saturday night. We will be there from Thursday morning til Sunday evening. Stop in anytime and if you need anything just ask.
>
> Just a reminder, the Saturday BBQ event is free for everyone, but we do ask for a donation, which again 100% goes to D.R.A.W. We collected over $1,400.00 last September, so let's do what we can to break the $1,500 mark this time.
>
> If you've visited us before, you know where we are. If you're new, we are located trackside just past the timing lights in the sportsman pits, camping sites 22 to 24. Look for the TEC banner.

<p class="post-source">Rodney Smith, in the club’s Facebook group</p>

The night beat that goal by a long way: $2,105, a new club record. [Read about spring 2017](/news/2017-spring-new-record/).
