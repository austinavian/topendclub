Hurricane Michael came through on race weekend, and the crowd was down by about a third. The night still raised $1,724 for D.R.A.W., which carried the club's all-time giving well past $10,000. The leftover food fed the RFC chaplains and their wives on Sunday, and the Safety Safari after the race.

## The numbers

- **Raised on the night** $1,724
- **Given to D.R.A.W. so far** more than $10,000

## From Rodney

> Another race, another Top End Memorial BBQ done. Seems as if Hurricane Michael might have kept some away. I hope everyone in his path is safe.
>
> Our attendance was off at least a third from previous events and we had a ton of food left over, which was OK. We had lunch Sunday, fed our RFC chaplains and wives, and carried a feast to the Safety Safari for supper after the race.
>
> Proud to say that even though attendance was down, we were still able to give D.R.A.W. $1,724.00, which is only some $500 less than our highest record. Thanks to everyone who gave and helped our favorite charity. This race put us waaaay over the $10K mark for donations since we started this.
>
> Thanks to everyone who has attended and given. Thanks to everyone who has helped and freely given their time over the years: Gerald Bridges, Gabe Bridges, Phil Blackburn, Mike and Carol Snyder, Mooch and Ken Berger.
>
> Finally, I would like to thank two special people who are not only special friends, but have stepped up as sponsors of the Top End Club: "Mr. Top Dragster" Alan O'Brien, and Super Gas racer and owner of Weaver's Automotive, Mr. Bernard Weaver. Thank you so much for the support, but thanks even more for the friendship. We sincerely love you both.
