Three weeks before the spring cookout, the Top End Club has a new logo and a full set of brand assets. It is the club's first redesign since [2017](/news/2017-new-logo/).

## The logo

The badge keeps the oval and the dragster that have stood for the club since 2014. Now the oval has a polished gold frame around a deep red gradient, and the dragster is cut in gold. The type moves out of the badge into its own wordmark: TOP END in Charlotte Italic, with Club in script.

## The full set

The new kit comes in four layouts: horizontal, stacked, the icon on its own, and the wordmark. The colors are Charlotte Red (#891511) and Concord Gold (#DA872D), with a red gradient and a gold gradient. Headings use Charlotte Italic, and everything else uses Avian Pro.

The full-color logos keep every detail of the gold and shading for big prints, banners and screens. The flat logos come in red, gold, black and white for shirts, stickers, signs and anything printed in one or two colors. There are matching name tags for the crew too.

Every logo is free to download for club events, sponsors and press on the [Branding page](/branding/).
