Rosalee and Terry Noble spent decades volunteering for D.R.A.W., running its booth at national events across the country, usually with little recognition. At the spring BBQ, the Top End Club said thank you with a blanket made of photos of the Nobles, the club, D.R.A.W. and the racing family they share. The idea was Jerry Bishop's.

## From Rodney

> Those of you who don't know Rosalee Noble and Terry Noble, you need to make a special effort to get to know them. They have devoted countless hours and years volunteering their time to D.R.A.W. at various national events to run the booth. They've done so much for so many over the years with little to no recognition for their efforts.
>
> A couple months back, Jerry Bishop called me with an idea he had, and we put together some pics that we thought represented the Nobles, the TEC, D.R.A.W., and our racing family. It turned out great, and we hope that in the days and years to come, when they look at this blanket, they smile and know we love them.

<p class="post-source">Rodney Smith, in the club’s Facebook group</p>
