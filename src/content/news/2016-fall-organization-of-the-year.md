Two pieces of good news came out of the fall race. Saturday night raised $1,425 for D.R.A.W., and on Friday evening Rosalee Noble told the club that D.R.A.W. had picked the Top End Club as its 2016 Organization of the Year. The award is presented at the D.R.A.W. charity auction in Dallas on October 15.

## The numbers

- **Raised on the night** $1,425
- **Given to D.R.A.W. so far** $2,885

## From Rodney

> For anyone not at the Top End Club BBQ Saturday, I want to share the great news we got Friday evening from Rosalee Noble and D.R.A.W. Every year D.R.A.W. recognizes an organization and honors them with an award for their efforts in giving back to the racing community and volunteering their time. We were floored when Rosalee announced that the D.R.A.W. 2016 Organization of the Year award goes to the Top End Club.
>
> The award will be presented Saturday, October 15, at the D.R.A.W. charity auction at the Dallas race. Looks like Tim Ridge and Jerry Bishop are going to make the trip. A huge thank you to Team Skillman for offering to make us their special guests and taking care of us while there. Great group there and we love 'em all.
>
> Thanks to everyone that was there Saturday night and helped us again raise $1,425.00. You Top Enders are a great bunch! Let's break $1,500 in April.

<p class="post-source">Rodney Smith, in the club’s Facebook group</p>
