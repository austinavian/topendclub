The fall cookout beat the spring record. Saturday night raised $2,160 for D.R.A.W., and after four Mark Lyle and Ronnie Davis Memorial BBQs, the club has now given more than $7,100.

## The numbers

- **Raised on the night** $2,160
- **Given to D.R.A.W. so far** about $7,150

## From Rodney

> This is what the Top End Club is all about. I know we started for a different reason, but it humbles us to see what it has become. Donations for D.R.A.W. totaled $2,160.00. Thanks for everyone's support. Without you there would not be a TEC.

<p class="post-source">Rodney Smith, in the club’s Facebook group</p>
