After the spring race, Rosalee Noble sent the club D.R.A.W.'s running count. In seven benefit nights since April 2016, the Top End Club has given $12,582.

## The numbers

- **Given to D.R.A.W. so far** $12,582
- **Club record for one night** $2,160

## From Rodney

> I have a couple things I would like to share with the group. First, I want to thank everyone who came out and supported D.R.A.W. and the TEC over the weekend. Second, I want to apologize to everyone there Saturday night for holding things up till 8:30. I promise it won't happen again. I realize some had to leave before we started serving food, and again I am sorry.
>
> The good news I want to share is, yesterday I received a text message from Rosalee Noble with the donation totals since we started this in April 2016. Through seven races now, you have donated a grand total of $12,582.00. I applaud you all for caring and giving. On behalf of Tim, Jerry, James and myself, we want to thank everyone for making this possible.
>
> October will be here before you know it and we'll do it all over again. I'd like to see our all-time record of $2,160.00 shattered, so put out the word and get out your wallets. LOL
