The fall cookout was dedicated to Rosalee Noble, who kept D.R.A.W.'s count of every dollar the club gave. In her memory, Saturday night raised $8,880 from the auction, the donation box and card receipts, a club record, and carried the club past $50,000 given.

## The numbers

- **Raised on the night** $8,880
- **Given to D.R.A.W. so far** $50,763

## From Rodney

> Here's the post that we as a group are so proud of. After all the money from the auction, donation box and card receipts was counted, a total of $8,880.00 was raised Saturday night for D.R.A.W. to help injured racers. Money was donated in the memory of Miss Rosalee Noble, as will all future donations.
>
> Thanks to everyone that made this happen. This puts our all-time donations at $50,763.00. Amazing people surround us.
