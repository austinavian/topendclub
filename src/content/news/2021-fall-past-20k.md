The fall event was again a private night, open mainly to sponsors and invited teams and guests, and planned on a smaller scale in case COVID-19 rules changed. It still took in more than expected: $2,363 for D.R.A.W., which carried the club past its $20,000 goal.

## The numbers

- **Raised on the night** $2,363
- **Given to D.R.A.W. so far** $20,005

## From Rodney

> Great event that came off fantastic. Very little leftovers or waste, and we actually took in more than expected to surpass our goal of $20K. Final tally was $2,363.00, which made our total-to-date donations $20,005. Thanks to you, our TEC family and friends, for your support. Without you there would be no Top End.

<p class="post-source">Rodney Smith, in the club’s Facebook group</p>
