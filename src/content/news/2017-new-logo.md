The Top End Club has a new logo for the 2017 season. When the club shared its first brand sheet in 2016, it said a major logo and branding overhaul was coming in 2017. This is it, in time for the spring Memorial BBQ.

## Where it started

The first logo was used from 2014 through 2016. It is an oval badge with a top-down dragster as the T between TOP and END, flames off the engine, THE across the blower and Club in script at the bottom. It came in Club Red (#4e0c0c) and Club Gold (#e1b585), in a full-color version, a version with an outline for dark backgrounds, and one-color versions.

## What changed

The new badge keeps the oval and the dragster, and makes both stronger. The dragster is redrawn with its roll cage and a three-ring blower. TOP END is set in heavy italic letters, Club sweeps through the middle in script, and EST. 2012 sits on the rear wing. The red is brighter and textured, and the colors have new names: Syndicate Red (#971e12) and Crew Gold (#d1b486).

## Three versions

The full-color logo with every detail is the main one, for large prints. The two-color badge drops the type for small sizes, where the letters would be hard to read. A one-color version is made for embroidered shirts and hats.

The 2017 logo carried the club for nine years, until the [2026 redesign](/news/2026-new-brand/).
