The club came back at full size for the 4-Wide Nationals, and Sunday morning's count shattered the old record. Saturday night raised $3,510 for D.R.A.W., and an uncounted $40 turned up from last fall.

## The numbers

- **Raised on the night** $3,510
- **Given to D.R.A.W. so far** $23,555

## From Rodney

> Wow, what a weekend, what a race, and WHAT A BENEFIT BBQ!
>
> Sunday morning, as we dragged ourselves out into the sunshine and started preparing to tear down the hospitality area, we sat down at a table and dreamed of what the donation box held. Even though it contained only paper, it felt slightly heavy, but then again, how heavy is loosely packed paper money?
>
> I unlocked the box and peeped inside, and I remember thinking that we could be disappointed, but then again, anything we get for our favorite charity is a blessing. So we started separating the different denominations into stacks and counted each stack. When the math was done and I hit the equal button on my phone, it absolutely blew me away. I grabbed Tim Ridge around the neck and cried (imagine that).
>
> Top End people, you need to pat yourselves on the back. You did this. You shattered our previous record. We just gave you the avenue. From Tim, James, Jerry, Gerald and myself, we want to say thank you for raising $3,510.00.
>
> Our total to date now (including a previously uncounted $40 from last September) is $23,555. Everyone involved should be proud of what's been accomplished. Thank you all, and we look forward to seeing and serving you again in September. We already have plans for a couple of surprises.
>
> Thanks again to our sponsors: Greenbrier Excavating (Alan O'Brien), Weaver's Racing Transmissions (Bernard Weaver), Auto Service Experts (John Ostendorf), C&M Automotive (Mitzi Black and Charlie West), Drink Hard Racing (Jim Parks, Thomas Wooden and Michael Cope), RPM Ministries (Beth and Mickey Prestridge), AllTech Sign Service (Florence and Jim Gottmann), Adkins Rentals (Jeff Adkins), Jason Stewart and Larry Wiggins Jr. Each one keeps this rolling on. Thank you.
