The club opened its new home this spring, sites 13 and 14 in the Sportsman pits, right ahead of the timing lights. The night's donations brought the club's all-time giving to D.R.A.W. close to the $10,000 mark.

## From Rodney

> What an awesome weekend. Every race my family seems to grow bigger and closer than before. The outpouring of love for our organization and the D.R.A.W. charity is overwhelming. Donations for this event put our all-time total closer to the ten thousand dollar mark. Thanks to everyone for their support, and thanks to those who always step up and help out on Saturday when it gets crunch time and we hit the panic button.
>
> I'm sorry I didn't take many pics this time. Got so tied up with my PR duties, as Tim says, that I let it slip my mind. Did manage to get a few, so I hope others took some and will share them here.
