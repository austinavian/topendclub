Three acts played the fall cookout: The Rockaholics, Jimmy Freeman and Sammy Eubanks. The weather kept some folks away, but the night raised $4,398. When the club took the money to D.R.A.W., Tim Ridge pulled out two dollars to make it an even $4,400.

## The numbers

- **Raised on the night** $4,400
- **Given to D.R.A.W. so far** $35,778

## From Rodney

> Another Top End Club event in the books, and although there was a hiccup or two, we stayed pretty much on the schedule we set up. The weather played its hand and kept some folks away, but we still had a great turnout. Donations were down a bit from the spring, and that was a hard one to beat. Still, we raised $4,398.00. When we took the money to D.R.A.W., Tim Ridge pulled out two bucks to make it an even $4,400.
>
> Thanks to everyone who came out and opened your hearts and wallets to support us and D.R.A.W.! This puts our all-time total at $35,778.00.
