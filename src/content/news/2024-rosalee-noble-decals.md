After Rosalee Noble passed away, Terry Noble sent the Top End Club a set of decals in her memory. The club offered them for a small donation, and, as always, every dollar went to D.R.A.W. in Rosalee's memory.

## From Rodney

> Just received these decals from Mr. Terry Noble. All proceeds will be given to D.R.A.W. in Rosalee's memory. Let's get them on carts, vehicles, campers, race cars, or whatever you want. Hit me up and let's keep Rosie's memory alive.

<p class="post-source">Rodney Smith, in the club’s Facebook group</p>

That fall, the club's cookout was dedicated to Rosalee and raised a record $8,880. [Read about fall 2024](/news/2024-fall-remembering-rosalee/).
