The fall race is almost here, and the Top End Club has the whole weekend planned. The goal for Saturday night: help the club break $15,000 given to D.R.A.W.

## The weekend

- **Thursday** Set up by lunch, sponsor dinner at night
- **Friday** Racing, friends, and the Smiling Dogs band
- **Saturday** TEC BBQ with Joe and the Big Dogs

## From Rodney

> 4 sleeps and wake up. Come on Thursday. Should be set up and ready for fun about lunchtime or shortly after. Come on by and check in. Thursday evening we'll be hosting dinner for our sponsors. Friday is chill day. Enjoy some racing and hang out with friends. Friday night it's Jim and Florence Gottmann's place for the Smiling Dogs band. Saturday is the big day: TEC BBQ bash with music from Joe and the Big Dogs.
>
> Remember it's a donation event, with proceeds benefiting D.R.A.W. Help us break the $15k donation mark. See everyone there!

<p class="post-source">Rodney Smith, in the club’s Facebook group</p>

They did: Saturday raised a record $2,560 and took the club past $15,000. [Read about fall 2019](/news/2019-fall-past-15k/).
