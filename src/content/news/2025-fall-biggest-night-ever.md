The final fall event was also a celebration of Rosalee Noble's life, with Jack Beckman and Jim Campbell running the live auction. The club hoped to pass $60,000 given and dreamed of $70,000. When the auction and donation box were counted, the night had raised $29,350, by far the biggest in club history.

## The numbers

- **Raised on the night** $29,350
- **Given to D.R.A.W. so far** $88,413

## From Rodney

> WHAT A NIGHT!!! I think I'm still in shock. Couldn't sleep last night, even after being up from 6 a.m. till 2 a.m. after the auction. Epic night, with a great turnout and some big hearts that made this the biggest event ever.
>
> Going into this race, we had an all-time donation total of $59,063, and we had a goal to easily break $60K and were hoping to set the bar at $70K. Well, we never imagined what was going to take place. The auction and donation box totals for the night ended at $29,350. That's crazy. I still can't believe it. That puts us at $88,413 total to date. UNBELIEVABLE!
>
> I think Rosalee would be very proud. Thanks to everyone that came and was a part of this record-breaking night, and thanks to all of our sponsors who make it possible.

<p class="post-source">Rodney Smith, in the club’s Facebook group</p>
