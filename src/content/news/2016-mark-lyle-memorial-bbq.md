For four years the Top End Club has cooked for whoever stopped by its pit on race weekend. This spring, it will cook for a cause. The club's Saturday night party at the 4-Wide Nationals will be the TEC Mark Lyle Memorial BBQ, with donations taken all night and every dollar given to D.R.A.W., the Drag Racing Association of Women, which helps injured racers and their families.

## From Rodney

> Well, the TEC brain trust got together Saturday night and made plans for the race. This year the party will be the TEC Mark Lyle Memorial BBQ. As suggested, we will be taking donations and presenting all proceeds to D.R.A.W. in honor of Mark. Safety Safari people, make sure you spread the word to your coworkers and NHRA officials.
>
> Let's make this great and help a racing family charity in honor of a fallen friend.

<p class="post-source">Rodney Smith, in the club’s Facebook group</p>

The night became the Mark Lyle and Ronnie Davis Memorial BBQ, and it raised $1,460. [Read how it went](/news/2016-spring-first-draw-benefit/).
