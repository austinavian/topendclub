The Top End Club is coming back to zMAX for the 4-Wide Nationals, its first race weekend since fall 2019. With state and track COVID-19 rules still in place, the Saturday benefit will not run at full size. Instead, the club will host a private event, open mainly to sponsors and invited teams and guests, with about a quarter of the usual food. Donations still go to D.R.A.W.

## The weekend

- **Thursday** Sponsor dinner, from about 6 PM
- **Friday** Burgers and hot dogs for supper
- **Saturday** Dinner and donations for D.R.A.W.

## From Rodney

> Since we are still dealing with COVID restrictions, we will not be able to have our normal Saturday benefit at full scale. State and track restrictions are a big pain, but we will still have the TEC operational, just on a smaller scale.
>
> We have provided lots of food during the good times and have given lots to D.R.A.W. Please help us continue to give even in these hard times. Show our sponsors that you support us now, and hopefully we will be back to normal for the fall race.

<p class="post-source">Rodney Smith, in the club’s Facebook group</p>

The private night raised $2,500. [Read about spring 2021](/news/2021-spring-back-at-the-track/).
