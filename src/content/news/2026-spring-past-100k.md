The 4-Wide Nationals brought the club's only event of 2026, and its biggest goal yet: $100,000 given to D.R.A.W. all-time. The night needed just under $12,000 to get there.

## The night

Rain and wind rolled in about an hour before the start, but things calmed down in time. Jack Beckman and Christine Holze arrived just as the welcome began, Brother Tom Ratliff asked the blessing, and the early end to racing brought a big crowd under the tents. Jeff Collins ran the live auction after dinner, and everything sold.

## The finish line

Sunday's count came up just $910 short of the goal. Then Jerry Meddock of Redline Sports Media introduced Rodney to Paul Miller of the Oliver & Miller fuel altered team, who covered the last $910 at the D.R.A.W. booth. The club's all-time giving passed $100,000.

## The numbers

- **Raised on the night** $11,592
- **Given to D.R.A.W. so far** more than $100,000

## From Rodney

> The 4-Wides have come and gone, and with it, sadly, our only event for 2026. Great weekend weather until Saturday evening, about an hour before we were to begin. The rain and wind came and we all were getting very anxious, especially me.
>
> Things calmed down for a bit and we were able to kick everything off as usual with a welcome and thanks given to all of our sponsors. Jack Beckman and Christine Holze dropped in, as always, just at the right time. These two are truly extraordinary human beings. Jack and I finished up the business side, and Brother Tom Ratliff asked the blessing on the food.
>
> Surprisingly, the light rain and early ending of racing brought us a fairly large crowd. Food service started a little late due to the weather, but the crowd stayed and everyone huddled under the tents and enjoyed a great meal, soon followed by the live auction. The crowd really showed out for the auctioneer, Mr. Jeff Collins. Everything sold and, to our surprise, netted a fairly large sum.
>
> Fast-forward to Sunday morning. Our goal was to top the $100K mark with this event. Our previous donation total was $88,413, which left us a little under $12K to reach that goal. We totaled all the money and came up short by $910.00. Yes, we were a little down, but hey, we took in over ten thousand dollars! Nothing to hang your head about.
>
> After a quick trip to the Beckman pit, as I was leaving, I ran into Jerry Meddock from Redline Sports Media, who introduced me to Paul Miller of the Oliver & Miller fuel altered racing team. After a brief conversation about the TEC and what we had done for D.R.A.W. over the years, Paul was amazed at the effort. He asked about paying by credit card, and I told him Steve Lesueur at the D.R.A.W. booth could take it. Paul said, "Come on, let's go make this happen." What a guy. He provided the $910.00 we needed to carry our all-time contributions past $100,000! That is simply amazing.
>
> Thanks to everyone who has supported us over the years, and again, thank you to every sponsor that keeps us moving forward.
>
> Sincerely, the Top End Club: Tim, Jerry, James L., James P., Gerald, Lee Roy and Rodney
