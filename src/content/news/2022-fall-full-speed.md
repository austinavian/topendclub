"Full. Speed." said the flyer, and the weekend lived up to it. Jimmy Freeman played, and Saturday night raised $2,665 for D.R.A.W.

## The numbers

- **Raised on the night** $2,665
- **Given to D.R.A.W. so far** $26,220

## From Rodney

> Another zMAX race has come and gone all too quickly again. Seems like we just got there and it was time to come home. Crazy wide-open weekend for sure, and some of it seems a blur. Thanks to everyone who came out Saturday night and supported our cause. Hope you enjoyed another TEC Memorial BBQ to benefit D.R.A.W.
>
> This installment netted $2,665.00 for our favorite charity and kicks our all-time total to $26,220.00.
>
> Thanks again to everyone, and thank you to all of our wonderful sponsors, without whom we wouldn't be able to do this. Once again I socialized too much and didn't take pics, so if you did, please share.
