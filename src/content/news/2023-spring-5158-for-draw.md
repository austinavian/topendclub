"Start your cookers," the flyer said. When the donation box and auction money were counted, the night came to $5,158 for D.R.A.W., a new club record by a wide margin.

## The numbers

- **Raised on the night** $5,158
- **Given to D.R.A.W. so far** $31,378

## From Rodney

> As we wrapped up the weekend's happenings and totaled the donation box and auction monies, we were thrilled to present D.R.A.W. with a total of $5,158.00. This will go a long way to help injured racers, and we thank you for giving.
>
> Our all-time total for donations now stands at $31,378.00. That, my friends, impresses me, and the thanks go to all of our TEC family.

<p class="post-source">Rodney Smith, in the club’s Facebook group</p>
