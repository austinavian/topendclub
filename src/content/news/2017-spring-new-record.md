The spring cookout was the biggest and best yet, and the donation box showed it. Saturday night raised $2,105 for D.R.A.W., beating the club's old record from its first benefit night.

## The numbers

- **Raised on the night** $2,105
- **Given to D.R.A.W. so far** $4,990

## From Rodney

> Spring event in the books. Thank you to everyone for making it the biggest and best yet. We were blown away by the generosity of everyone. Our D.R.A.W. donations shattered the previous record and totaled $2,105.00. Thank you, thank you for supporting such a great organization and supporting us.
>
> Special thanks go out to Ken Berger, Gerald Bridges, Allison Carter and Deanna Smith for helping pull it all together and get set up for Saturday. Thanks again to everyone, and now we have a huge goal to meet and/or beat in September.
