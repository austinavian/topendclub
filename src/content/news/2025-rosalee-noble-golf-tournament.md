Kendra Wolf and D.R.A.W. announced the first annual Rosalee Noble Memorial Golf Tournament, benefiting D.R.A.W. It was played on Thursday, September 18, at Rocky River Golf Club, right beside zMAX Dragway, the Thursday before the Carolina Nationals.

## From Rodney

> Do we have any golfers in the TEC? Is anyone interested in a golf tournament? Kendra Wolf, along with D.R.A.W., is proud to announce the First Annual Rosalee Noble Memorial Golf Tournament benefiting D.R.A.W. Get those clubs out and golf to support our favorite charity.

<p class="post-source">Rodney Smith, in the club’s Facebook group</p>

## How the TEC did

A foursome of Bruce Young, Jon Young, James L. Blackford and Keith Guffey, better known as the Redneck Golf Association, played for the Top End Club in the first tournament and finished in second place.
