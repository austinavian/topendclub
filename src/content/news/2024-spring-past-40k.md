Going into the spring race, Rodney set a goal: $40,000 given to D.R.A.W. all-time. Between the donation box and the auction, Saturday night raised $6,105, another record, and blew right past it.

## The numbers

- **Raised on the night** $6,105
- **Given to D.R.A.W. so far** $41,883

## From Rodney

> The results are in. All donations counted, and once again the bar height has been raised. Thanks to everyone who came and supported. Thanks to those who donated for the auction. And thanks to everyone who pitched in to make this weekend and the BBQ event run smooth.
>
> Going into this, I set a goal to hit the $40K mark in all-time donations. Y'all pitched in and blew that right out of the water. Everything from Saturday night amounted to a whopping $6,105.00 given to D.R.A.W.! That puts our all-time total at $41,883.00, just under the $42K mark. That just blows me away.
>
> We at the TEC, and I know D.R.A.W., would like to thank everyone who has had a hand in making this possible. Y'all keep coming and we will break down the $50K door next. As per the D.R.A.W. motto, that's a lot of Fast Help for Fast Friends!
>
> Ms. Rosalee and Terry Noble, I hope we made you proud! We love and miss you both.
