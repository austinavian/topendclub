The spring cookout added something new: a live auction after dinner. Between the auction and the donation box, Saturday night raised $8,300 for D.R.A.W., given in memory of Rosalee Noble.

## The numbers

- **Raised on the night** $8,300
- **Given to D.R.A.W. so far** $59,063

Thank you to everyone who bid, gave and helped, and to every sponsor on this spring's flyer. See you in September for the final fall event.
