After COVID-19 cancelled 2020, the club came back for the 4-Wide Nationals with a private night, open mainly to sponsors and invited teams and guests. The night raised $2,500 for D.R.A.W., given in honor of Phillip "Kilroy" Bishop.

## The numbers

- **Raised on the night** $2,500
- **Given to D.R.A.W. so far** $17,642

## From Rodney

> Proud to say that after the 2021 TEC spring event, we have donated a grand total of $17,642.00 to D.R.A.W. Thanks to everyone who has given to our favorite charity and helped us accomplish this.
>
> Looking forward to September. Let's kick down the door of $20K like we own it.
