Five months after the first benefit night, the Top End Club did it again. Saturday night at the fall race was the second Mark Lyle and Ronnie Davis Memorial BBQ, free to anyone who stopped by, with a donation box for D.R.A.W. It raised $1,425, close to the spring night's $1,460, and brought the club's giving in its first year to $2,885.

## The numbers

- **Raised on the night** $1,425
- **Given to D.R.A.W. so far** $2,885

## From Rodney

> Thanks to everyone that was there Saturday night and helped us again raise $1,425.00. You Top Enders are a great bunch! Let's break $1,500 in April.

<p class="post-source">Rodney Smith, in the club’s Facebook group</p>
